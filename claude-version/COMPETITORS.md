# Competitor scan - 02/11/26

Scope: Humphreys / Anjeong-ri / Songtan studios, Seoul private studios, top sacred-geometry artists' sites. Pitch-level scan, not exhaustive.

## 0. Blocker found: tattoo shops are off-limits to USFK personnel

- The official USAG Humphreys off-limits page (live, last modified 02/09/26) lists: **"All tattoo/body piercing shops IAW USFK 190-2 (Includes, but not limited to)"**, then 5 named Anjeong-ri shops (Get Inked, Rey Ink Master, Jimmy's TATTOO, TaTToo Story, TATTOO). The PDF of the list dated 17/12/25 says the same. The USFK-wide policy (USFK Instruction 5260.01) lists "All tattoo parlors and body piercing establishments."
- Gemini's whole strategy (military-friendly, AR 670-1 placement advice, PCS scheduling, "2 min from Gate 6") markets directly to people who are ordered not to go there.
- Risks of publishing that copy: it looks like soliciting off-limits personnel, and it could put Zee's studio on the named list.
- **Not verified:** whether the order also covers DoD civilians, contractors and family members, or only service members. That needs USFK 190-2 itself (the USFK page returned 403) or Zee's own experience.
- **Recommendation:** no military targeting in the copy. Audience = Korean locals + international residents and visitors (English-speaking) + Seoul/SRT collectors. Give the location as an address and map, not "for soldiers". Zee confirms how she handles base clients today.

## 1. Local market (Pyeongtaek / Anjeong-ri / Songtan)

- No local studio with a proper website surfaced; shops live on Instagram, Threads, Naver Place and directories. A clean bilingual site is a differentiator (based on search results only, not a full Naver audit).
- Several Anjeong-ri shops are named on the off-limits list for health/safety. Hygiene is a trust lever, but only with claims Zee can prove.

## 2. Seoul private studios (tone and booking models)

- **Seoul Memory Tattoo** ([koreatattoo.carrd.co](https://koreatattoo.carrd.co/)): one page, short warm copy ("Memories You Can Wear", "Real Results. Honest Work. No filters."), fresh vs healed photos, private 1:1, reservation only, Kakao open-chat link. Closest model for tone and simplicity.
- Top Hongdae artists (Hongdam, Sol): bookings open in windows through a Google Form, EN and KR. High demand, low friction.
- Aggregator studios (HTK, Ink Avenue): "send a booking request" forms.

## 3. Sacred-geometry reference (Dillon Forte, US)

- [dillonfortetattoo.com](https://www.dillonfortetattoo.com/): full-bleed photos of the work, a 3-line bio, one "BOOK NOW" button that opens an email. Minimal text, but the client has to write everything themselves.
- Lesson: let the work carry the page; but give the client a short guided message so the first DM is complete.

## 4. Takeaways for our build

1. Work first, words second. Full-bleed healed work, minimal copy.
2. Plain, warm tone (Seoul Memory model). No jargon, no gold-luxury cliche.
3. Booking = one screen: idea, placement, rough size, reference photos, then a ready message to Kakao / Instagram / email. Standard fields per industry guides; no budget or price field (Rasta: no prices).
4. Trust = "fresh vs healed" pairs + short hygiene list of facts Zee confirms.
5. No military marketing (section 0).
